# terco's XC functionals

GENERATED by `tools/gen_xc_registry.py` -- do not edit.

Every functional [ExchCXX](https://github.com/wavefunction91/ExchCXX) defines, plus terco's own local entries, is selectable by name through `xc_functional_by_name`. 69 run; 18 are registered but refuse to run, each for a stated reason.

Kernels: 87 translated by `tools/exchcxx_to_fortran.py`, of which 79 agree with libxc at check_xc's tolerances and 8 do not (listed under their functionals below); 16 refused outright.

Three of the 69 are double hybrids (b2plyp, b2gp-plyp, mpw2plyp): the table
below lists their SCF composition only (a_x exact exchange plus a GGA
exchange/correlation mix), not the RI-MP2 fraction on top, which is never a
kernel -- see `trc_xc_functional_t`'s own `pt2_cos`/`pt2_css` and
`trc_capi.F90`'s `trc_run_rimp2`/`trc_energy`.
